MEDSUPPLY AI - PROCUREMENT GUIDELINES

1.  INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

-   Maintain accurate records of medical equipment and essential
    supplies.
-   Define a minimum stock level for every critical item.
-   Review inventory regularly and identify items below the minimum
    stock level.
-   Estimate days of stock remaining using current stock and average
    daily usage.
-   Prioritize urgent shortages that may affect hospital operations.

2.  SUPPLIER EVALUATION

-   Compare quotations from multiple suppliers whenever possible.
-   Consider unit price, delivery time, product quality, reliability,
    and warranty.
-   Do not select a supplier based on price alone.
-   Verify supplier credentials and product certifications before
    procurement.

3.  PURCHASE PLANNING

-   Calculate suggested purchase quantity based on the stock shortage
    and expected demand.
-   Check product specifications and compatibility before placing an
    order.
-   Confirm availability and delivery timelines with the selected
    supplier.
-   Keep a record of quotations and procurement decisions.

4.  QUALITY AND SAFETY CHECKS

-   Check expiry dates for applicable medical products.
-   Inspect products for damage and verify that delivered quantities
    match the purchase order.
-   Confirm required certifications, warranty, and quality
    documentation.
-   Follow the hospital’s approved storage and handling procedures.

5.  APPROVAL AND AUDIT

-   All purchase recommendations must be reviewed by an authorized
    hospital procurement officer.
-   Do not automatically place purchase orders based only on an AI
    recommendation.
-   Record approvals, invoices, supplier details, and procurement
    decisions for audit purposes.
-   Follow applicable hospital policies and local procurement
    regulations.

IMPORTANT These guidelines are sample educational content for the
MedSupply AI project. They are not a substitute for official hospital
policies, clinical advice, or applicable regulations.
